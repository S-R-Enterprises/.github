</div>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%" valign="top">

### ⚡ We are Spaars.

**Spaars** is a hackathon team built around one simple idea:

> **Don't just talk about the idea. Build it.**

We come together to turn rough ideas into **working prototypes under pressure**.

Hackathons give us limited time, limited resources, and a problem to solve.

We use that pressure to experiment, learn unfamiliar technologies, collaborate, and ship.

We don't always know the technology before we start.

**That's part of the fun.**
